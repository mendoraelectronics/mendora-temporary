Meet the tool

Complex repairs.
A more connected toolkit.

Electronic repair often means moving between separate tools and manual troubleshooting. Mendora is developing a multi-functional UART-based tablet to bring key diagnostic and programming workflows together.

01 / DIAGNOSE

Understand the fault.

UART-based access to processor information and boot logs is being developed to help technicians investigate firmware and hardware faults.

UART accessMulti-mode diagnostics
02 / RECOVER

Bring firmware back.

Firmware reading, writing, verification and recovery workflows are being brought together to support repairs across compatible embedded electronic systems.

Read & writeVerify & recover
03 / PROGRAM

Do more with one tool.

Multi-platform EEPROM programming and cloning are part of the development scope, reducing the need to switch between separate programming tools.

EEPROM programmingFirmware cloning
Development status

A working vision.
A prototype in progress.

The ULTRA UART AI REPAIR TOOL is currently at the prototype development stage and undergoing testing. Our focus is on validating the integrated repair workflows before moving towards field use.

Current stage: Prototype development

Capabilities described here reflect the product’s development scope. Compatibility, performance and final specifications remain subject to testing and validation.

03 — FUTURE DIRECTION
